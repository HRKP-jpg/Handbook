Confidentiality & Data Security

Protecting client and company information is a fundamental responsibility. All data must be treated as confidential and handled with the highest level of care at all times.

General Guidelines

Never share passwords, files, or confidential information without proper authorization.

Always log out of systems and tools when they are not in use.

Immediately report any unusual or suspicious activity to HR or your Team Leader.

Do’s

Use only company-approved tools and platforms.

Store files exclusively in the client’s designated cloud storage.

Report any security concerns or suspected data breaches immediately.

Don’ts

Do not use personal devices or personal storage for work files.

Do not disclose client or company information to unauthorized persons.

Do not capture or send screenshots containing sensitive or confidential data.

Employment and Probation

All newly hired Virtual Assistants are subject to a three-month probationary period.

During probation:

Performance, attendance, and workplace behavior are closely monitored and evaluated on an ongoing basis.

The “No Work, No Pay” policy is strictly implemented, including during statutory holidays.

Employees who are on probationary status are not yet eligible for paid statutory holidays.

Important Clarification on Pay & Leave

All payments are processed under KeyPlayers

Clients are aware that:

  • During probation, leaves are unpaid
  • Exceptions may occur if the client voluntarily offers payment

Do not initiate or request paid leave during probation

Upon successful completion of probation, employees become eligible for company benefits and paid holidays per policy.

Regularization Policy

Regularization of Virtual Assistants (VAs) is determined on a per-client basis. A VA becomes regular after successfully completing the required probationary period with a specific client.

However, when a VA is assigned to a new client, they will automatically return to probationary status for that new engagement.

In summary:

  • Regularization is client-specific, not contract-based.
  • A VA will undergo a probationary period for each new client assignment.
  • Being a regular VA with one client does not carry over to another client.

Attendance & Time-Off Policy

Leave Credits (Annual Allocation)

The following leave credits are provided annually:

  • 10 Vacation Leave Days
  • 5 Sick Leave Days
  • 5 Emergency Leave Days

Holiday Observance

We do not observe Philippine holidays

VAs are expected to work on North American statutory holidays by default, unless the client advises otherwise

The only exceptions are New Year’s Day and Christmas Day, which are automatic days off

Statutory Holiday Policy

If a client does not require a Virtual Assistant (VA) to work on a statutory holiday, the VA must still submit a leave request. The day will be considered a client-approved non-working day.

For regular VAs, approved statutory holidays are treated as paid time off and will not be deducted from leave credits.

For probationary VAs, statutory holidays are unpaid (No Work, No Pay) unless stated otherwise by the client.

Emergency Leave (EL) Policy

Emergency Leave (EL) may be used for the following situations:

Power Outages: Unexpected power outages that prevent the EA from performing their duties.

Medical Emergencies: Urgent medical situations, such as hospitalization or other serious medical emergencies.

Death of an Immediate Family Member: EAs may use Emergency Leave for the first day of absence in the event of the death of an immediate family member. Please note that KeyPlayers HQ does not have a separate bereavement leave policy.

Documentation Requirements:

Valid supporting documentation must be submitted for all Emergency Leave requests, where applicable.

For medical emergencies, medical records or other appropriate medical documentation must be provided.

All Emergency Leave requests are subject to HR review and approval.

Planned Leave

  • 1–2 days: Request 2 weeks in advance
  • 3 or more days: Request 1 month in advance
  • Notify HR and your Team Leader via email
  • Send a Slack or WhatsApp message for visibility

Mandatory Requirement:

  • All leave requests must be submitted through the official Leave Request Form
  • This applies to all types of leave, including statutory holidays
  • Requests will not be considered valid without a completed form

Unplanned Leave

  • Notify HR or your Team Leader at least 2 hours before your shift
  • A follow-up email is required for documentation

Mandatory Requirement:

  • The Leave Request Form must still be completed after notification
  • This ensures proper documentation and approval of the leave

Terminal Leave During the Resignation Period

EAs are not permitted to use their remaining leave during the resignation notice period, subject to applicable legal requirements and company policy.

During the rendering period, EAs are expected to fulfill their responsibilities and provide the necessary support to clients.

EAs must complete the required knowledge transfer, assist with the transition to the incoming VA, and submit all necessary handover documentation before their final working day.

These requirements help ensure continuity of client services and a smooth transition of responsibilities.

No Call / No Show (NCNS)

  • Strict zero-tolerance policy
  • May result in disciplinary action or termination

Resignation Policy

EAs must submit a formal written resignation notice at least two (2) weeks before their intended final working day.

All required handovers, access removals, and documentation must be completed before departure.

Knowledge Transfer documents and Training Manuals must be submitted before the completion of the offboarding process.

Immediate Resignation

Immediate resignation cases will be reviewed in accordance with company policy and applicable legal requirements.

Failure to comply with the required resignation notice period or offboarding procedures may be subject to further review.

If an immediate resignation is due to a medical condition, the EA must submit supporting medical documentation, such as a medical certificate, medical abstract, or other relevant medical records, for HR review.

HR will assess the circumstances and supporting documentation before determining the appropriate next steps.

Final Pay

Final pay will be processed in accordance with company policy and applicable legal requirements.

Any outstanding payments, deductions, or other financial consequences related to the resignation will be reviewed in accordance with applicable laws and contractual obligations.

Time Tracking & Payment (Scrin.io)

Scrin.io is the tool used to track work hours. Virtual Assistants (VAs) must follow these guidelines to ensure accurate time records and payment.

General Rules

  • Scrin.io must remain running throughout your shift.
  • Only verified and approved work hours are eligible for payment, in accordance with company policy.
  • Logging out of Scrin.io during your shift without authorization may result in discrepancies in recorded hours and possible disciplinary action.
  • Do not delete, alter, or manipulate screenshots or tracking records.
  • Keep all notes clear, accurate, and professional.

Important System Reminder

If your screen becomes idle or turns off:

  • Scrin.io may automatically log you out.
  • Scrin.io may log you back in once your screen becomes active.

To Avoid Missing Hours

  • Avoid leaving your screen idle for extended periods while working.
  • Ensure your device remains active during your shift, in accordance with company procedures.
  • Monitor Scrin.io regularly to ensure your work hours are being recorded correctly.

Screenshots & Notes

  • Keep all records accurate, complete, and professional.
  • Screenshots must not be deleted, as they may be used for audits, investigations, or time-tracking reviews.

If Issues Occur

  • Report any time-tracking issues immediately to your Team Leader or HR.
  • Provide screenshots or other supporting evidence whenever possible.
  • Do not delete, or manipulate tracking records.
  • Wait for further instructions from your Team Leader or HR before attempting any corrective action.

Compliance with this policy helps protect both EAs and the company by supporting accurate time records and payment processing.
